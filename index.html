Questions

Is Nudge free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Will it work if my phone restarts or is on silent?

Yes. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and come back after a restart. For the most reliable timing on Android 14 and later, allow "Alarms & reminders" when Nudge asks.

What happens if I take only some of my pills?

Tap More, then Took some, and tick the ones you took. Nudge logs a partial dose and adjusts your stock to match.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Nudge has no servers and no accounts. You can export a backup file at any time from Settings.

Is there an iPhone version?

An iPhone version is in testing. Android comes first.

Does Nudge give medical advice?

No. Nudge helps you remember your medication. Always follow the advice of your GP or pharmacist.
