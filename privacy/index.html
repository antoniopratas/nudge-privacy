Nudge privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Nudge is a medication reminder app made by Antonio Pratas. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. Nudge has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads, and it does not collect, sell or share your data.

What Nudge stores

To work, Nudge stores the following on your device only:

  • Your medicines (name, strength, form, colour, dose, stock and refill settings)
  • Your routines (reminder times, days and repeat settings)
  • Your dose history (taken, skipped, partial or not tracked) and snoozes
  • Optional photos of medicine packaging that you take or choose
  • Your settings, such as your name, theme and reminder preferences

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: to remind you when a dose is due. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to add a packaging photo.
  • Run at start-up: so reminders keep working after your phone restarts.

Backups and sharing

If you use Nudge's backup or share features, the app creates a file and hands it to the app you choose (for example email or cloud storage). What happens to that file is then up to you and that app. Your phone's own system backup (such as Google backup on Android) may also include Nudge's data, under your Google or Apple account settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time by clearing Nudge's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. As we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

Nudge is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Medical disclaimer

Nudge is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
